Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to ASHFUN. We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about how information is handled when you visit our website. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it may be used.

1. Information We Collect

ASHFUN is designed to provide small browser-based games, interactive experiences and entertainment. We do not currently ask visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to use the main experiences.

Our website hosting provider may automatically process basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, requested pages and server logs, to deliver and protect the website.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use essential technical features required for its operation. We do not currently operate our own analytics cookie system.

If we introduce analytics or other third-party services in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant technologies and choices.

3. Third-Party Services

Some website resources may be provided by third parties, such as font providers or website hosting services. Those providers may process technical information when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies govern their services.

4. How We Use Information

Technical information processed by our hosting provider may be used to:

  • Keep the website available and working.
  • Detect errors, abuse and security issues.
  • Understand and troubleshoot technical problems.

5. Data Retention and Security

Technical logs may be retained by our hosting provider according to its own retention and security practices. We do not currently maintain a user account database for the website.

6. Children's Privacy

ASHFUN is a general-audience entertainment website. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information processed about you. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Third-party services may provide additional privacy controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website changes, including when new features or third-party services are introduced. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices on ASHFUN, please contact the website operator. Contact details will be added here when an official contact address is established.
